Privacy & your signup

Your signup is visible to organizers. Sign in again to change or delete it.

Signing in doesn’t submit a response or request a button. We don’t post, follow, or send automatic messages. Choosing “Not this time” cancels your button request, not your future-meetup preference.

If you choose future meetups, organizers keep your Bluesky account and opt-in date to contact you about another meetup. No email. Uncheck it and save to unsubscribe, whether or not you’re coming in November.

Deleting your signup removes your attendance, saved artwork and future-meetup preference, but not copies already downloaded or printed. A hashed account marker remains to prevent old tabs restoring deleted requests. Signing out doesn’t delete your signup.
